## Why e-commerce specifically

Google tied Core Web Vitals to ranking long ago, but for e-commerce this isn't about SEO — it's about money. Every 100ms of delay on a mobile storefront means measurable percentages of abandoned sessions. Three metrics, in the places where they hurt conversion most.

## LCP — the product page

Largest Contentful Paint on a product page is almost always the hero image. Typical mistakes: the image lazy-loads (yes, people lazy-load the main photo too), no `fetchpriority="high"`, PNG instead of AVIF/WebP.

What I do in projects:

- Hero image — `priority`, `fetchpriority="high"`, never lazy-loaded
- AVIF with WebP fallback, `srcset` for three breakpoints
- Preconnect to the image CDN — saves 100–300ms on handshakes

Target: LCP ≤ 2.0s on mobile 4G. Achievable without a single design compromise.

## INP — catalog filters

Interaction to Next Paint measures UI responsiveness. A catalog with 500+ SKUs and filters is the classic case where every checkbox click recomputes everything and freezes the main thread.

Techniques:

- Filtering inside `startTransition` — the UI stays responsive while React re-renders the list
- Virtualization for long lists (or honest pagination — it's underrated)
- Frame-based debouncing: one `requestAnimationFrame` per burst of clicks

A good target is INP ≤ 200ms. Users should never watch the interface "think".

## CLS — checkout

Cumulative Layout Shift at checkout isn't aesthetics — it's clicks on the wrong buttons. The culprits are always the same: late-loading installment banners, delivery widgets, web fonts without `size-adjust`.

The recipe is boring and effective: reserve space for every async block, `font-display: swap` with a metric-matched fallback, skeletons instead of spinners — they hold the page geometry together.

## What to measure with

Lab data (Lighthouse) is for development. Field data (CrUX, RUM) is the truth. Watch the 75th percentile on mobile, not the average: averages lie, and the distribution tail is your real customers on weak devices.

**Bottom line.** Web Vitals isn't a checkbox list — it's three specific places in your funnel. Product page, catalog, checkout — close them, and conversion metrics respond before you finish writing the report.
