## Problem

Charging-network websites look alike: dark background, neon, pin map. The goal was to escape the template and create a visual metaphor that ties the product to the physics of the process — charging as filling a vessel with energy.

## Solution

The **Liquid Charge** concept: a dark vessel slowly filled with glowing liquid. A Canvas particle field flows toward the connector, a fixed level indicator tracks scroll progress, and CTAs breathe at 60 BPM.

## Inside

- **Hero** — connector in negative space, energy particles
- **Stats** — 4 network counters
- **Stations** — 6 cards with hover spotlight
- **Calculator** — sliders for mileage, petrol price, and tariff → monthly savings
- **Tariffs** — Pay Go / Subscriber / Fleet
- **App** — CSS phone mockup of a charging session
- **FAQ** — accordion
- **Fleet** — fleet inquiry form

## Stack

A single `index.html`: GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis, Canvas 2D particle field. Fonts: Chakra Petch, Inter, JetBrains Mono. Calculator, accordion, and form are vanilla JS.

## Result

A product landing page with its own motion character. QA covers particle canvas, liquid level, calculator, accordion, form, and overflow.
