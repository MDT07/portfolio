## Problem

Architecture websites often fall into one of two poles: faceless portfolio carousels or dry project tables. The goal was to create an experience that conveys scale, analytical rigor, and materiality at the same time.

## Solution

The **Sectional Monument** concept: a building is read through its cut. The site is built as a sequence of horizontal section bands. The hero separates facade and internal section via scroll-driven `clip-path`. The cursor becomes a drafting knife with coordinates, the material palette reacts to hover, and dimension lines draw themselves on scroll.

## Inside

- **Hero** — facade and section split by scroll; dimension annotations
- **Projects** — 3 section bands with asymmetric 60/40 grid
- **Materials** — interactive strip of 5 swatches
- **Services** — 4 text cards
- **Contact** — form with validation
- **Footer** — giant outline logotype

## Stack

A single `index.html`: GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis for scroll and reveals, CSS custom properties for tokens. Fonts: Outfit, Inter, JetBrains Mono. Cursor knife, material hovers, and SVG dimension animations are vanilla JS.

## Result

An architecture studio website with a recognizable section-cut signature. QA covers boot, fonts, clip-path, dimension lines, material strip, form, and horizontal overflow.
