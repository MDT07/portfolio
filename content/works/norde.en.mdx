## Problem

Standard clothing and homeware stores show a catalog but do not tell the story of a thing. For a niche Scandinavian brand, it is important to convey material origin, makers, and lifecycle — while keeping a working storefront.

## Solution

The **Object Biographies** concept: every object has a biography. The hero is a large specification card for the flagship. Followed by a manifest, a MapLibre provenance map, a material passport, an inventory ledger with accordion, a journal, and an archive club.

## Inside

- **Hero** — specimen card with object fields
- **Manifest** — 3 archive principles
- **Provenance map** — MapLibre with material routes
- **Material passport** — 4 cards with progress bars
- **Inventory ledger** — 8 objects, accordion, size selector, cart
- **Journal** — notes on materials
- **Archive club** — subscribe to passports of new objects

## Stack

A single `index.html`: GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis, MapLibre GL JS for the map, Howler for optional sound. Cart keyed by `id::size`, drawer with focus management. Fonts: Cormorant Garamond, Source Sans 3, IBM Plex Mono.

## Result

Editorial ecommerce that sells not just the product but its history. QA covers specimen card, map, ledger, cart, drawer, and accessibility.
