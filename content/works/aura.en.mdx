## Problem

Fintech landing pages are often overloaded with gradients, phone mockups, and empty slogans. The goal was to convey trust and speed through engineering aesthetics and live numbers rather than decoration.

## Solution

The **AURA** concept is a neobank as a trading terminal. Dark precision, monochrome base with an electric violet accent (#7C5CFF), engineered typography Sora + Inter + JetBrains Mono. The hero shows a kinetic balance card backed by Canvas spending rings that rotate on scroll.

## What's inside

- **Hero** — balance card with count-up amount and Canvas spending rings
- **Features** — 4 product cards
- **Card studio** — interactive color and material selector for the card
- **Converter** — currency exchange with mock rate and copy-to-clipboard
- **Security** — 3 security layers
- **Early access** — waitlist form

## Architecture & stack

Single `index.html`: GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis for scroll and reveals, CSS custom properties for tokens. Canvas 2D for spending rings, vanilla JS for the card studio, converter, and form. Fonts: Sora, Inter, JetBrains Mono.

## Result

A self-contained demo template for a neobank. QA verifies boot, fonts, Canvas rings, card studio, converter, and absence of horizontal overflow.
