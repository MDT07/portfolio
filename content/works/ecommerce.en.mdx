## Problem

Small clothing and homeware brands need a storefront on par with major retailers, but without the budget for a team or a platform. Off-the-shelf website-builder templates all look the same: cluttered product cards, aggressive discount badges, random typography — and they fail to sell the one thing a niche brand actually has: character.

## Solution

A demo storefront template called "NORDE" in a Scandinavian aesthetic: a monochrome dark base, a single signal accent, editorial product presentation, and a strict modular grid. A fully self-contained HTML file — it opens in a browser with no build step or server — while the entire purchase flow works end to end: from catalog filtering to checkout.

## What's inside

- **Header** — fixed, with a blur backdrop on scroll and a cart icon with a live counter
- **Hero** — large asymmetric typography, collection photo, CTA
- **Categories** — 4 cards that filter the catalog on click
- **Catalog** — 8 products with category filters and a hover "Quick view" action
- **Quick view** — a modal with description, size selection, and add-to-cart (closes on Esc)
- **Cart** — a right-side drawer: quantity controls, removal, delivery and total calculation, checkout
- **Benefits** — delivery, returns, warranty, and payment in strict text cards

## Architecture and stack

A single self-contained `index.html`: semantic markup, design tokens via CSS custom properties, and all interactivity in dependency-free vanilla JavaScript. Cart state lives in a JS object; catalog and cart rendering use markup-generator functions and event delegation. IntersectionObserver drives the reveal animations.

## Result

A storefront that can be shown to a client as a finished concept or used as a foundation: the color and typography tokens transfer to any design system, and the catalog and cart JS modules can be wired up to a real backend.
