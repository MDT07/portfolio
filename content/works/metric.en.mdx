## Problem

Small businesses need a CRM that doesn't force them into someone else's process. Off-the-shelf systems are either bloated or rigid: you can't quickly assemble your own section, your own pipeline, your own entity — for real estate, manufacturing, or an agency. The goal was to show a platform at the level of leading 2026 SaaS products, where a standard core combines with a blank-canvas builder — all running locally, with no backend.

## Solution

A single self-contained `index.html` — a full application in vanilla JS with its own state manager and localStorage persistence. The core: dashboard, deals, clients, tasks, analytics, invoices. On top — the builder: pages assembled from widgets on a drag-n-drop grid, and custom entities that automatically appear in the navigation with a table and CRUD. Data is seeded by a deterministic PRNG (mulberry32), so the demo looks identical on every open, while user edits persist on top.

## What's inside

- **Dashboard** — KPI cards with sparklines, an SVG revenue chart with crosshair and tooltip, sales funnel, live activity feed (simulated events every 42 seconds)
- **Deals** — kanban with pointer-events drag-n-drop, sortable table, a drawer with an AI next-step hint and notes
- **Clients** — companies and contacts, company profile with related deals and contacts
- **Tasks** — status columns, month calendar, overdue highlighting
- **Analytics** — conversion, average deal size, deal sources, team leaderboard
- **Invoices** — totals by status, status-change drawer, activity logging
- **Builder** — 7 widget types (KPI, chart, funnel, deals, tasks, note, progress), a 12-column grid with dragging, resize and collision checks; entity wizard with typed fields
- **METRIC AI** — chat assistant with a local intent engine: weekly summary, revenue and weighted forecast, pipeline status, overdue items, top deals, email drafts, creating tasks and deals by command
- **2026 interactions** — ⌘K command palette with data search, G→D/P/C/T/A/I/B/S shortcuts, toasts, empty states, demo reset

## Architecture and stack

Zero dependencies: semantic markup, design tokens via CSS custom properties (light and dark themes through `data-theme`), template-function rendering, a global `data-action` dispatcher, hash router. Charts are hand-built SVG with a hover crosshair. Kanban and builder-grid drag-n-drop run on pointer events with `elementFromPoint`. Dates and numbers use Intl — the RU/EN switch in the topbar translates the entire shell. The only external resources are Google Fonts (Onest + JetBrains Mono, Cyrillic coverage).

## Result

A CRM you can actually run as a working tool: every button works, every form persists, and the builder genuinely assembles new sections. Verified by a CDP run: 27 DOM assertions on desktop and 31 on mobile, clean console, with drag-n-drop, palette, AI and theme switching green on both form factors.
